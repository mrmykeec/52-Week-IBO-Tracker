# 52-Week IBO Tracker

A single-file HTML tracker built to support the Carrithers 52-week IBO 
training program. Designed for the Worldwide Group team.

## How it works

- No install. No accounts. No server.
- All data stays on the user's device via localStorage.
- Export/Import backup bridges the gap if the user switches devices or 
  clears their browser.

## Sharing

Open the link below in Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android) and add to your
